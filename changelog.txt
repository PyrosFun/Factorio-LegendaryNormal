Version: 1.0.4
Date: 02. 23. 2026
  Features:
    - Added Ignore Asteroids setting to restore asteroid stats to vanilla normal quality values.
    - Switched enemy stat normalization from runtime entity replacement to data-stage scaling reversal.
    - Added support for Gleba pentapods (stompers and strafers) in enemy ignore system.
    - Demolishers now correctly have their stats restored to vanilla values when the Ignore Enemies setting is enabled.
    - Added acid damage normalization for spitter and worm attacks (impact, puddle, and sticker damage).
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed Gleba spider-unit enemies (pentapods) not being handled by the Ignore Enemies setting.
    - Fixed electric mining drill resource drain not being patched (wrong internal entity name).
    - Fixed burner mining drill resource drain not being patched (missing from drill list).
    - Fixed electric and burner mining drills using incorrect drain values (were using big mining drill values).
